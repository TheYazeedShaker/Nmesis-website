Give the space a purpose.

A virtual showroom should do more than place cars in a digital room. It should help visitors understand the range and find the vehicle they want to explore.

Build a clear path through the range.

Navigation, model information and points of interest work together. The experience should make it clear where to go next without interrupting the vehicle itself.

Connect the experience to the brief.

The right content and integrations depend on the brand, market and intended customer journey. Those requirements should be agreed before the environment and interactions are built.
