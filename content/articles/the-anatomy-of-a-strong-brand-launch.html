Start with the vehicle.

A useful vehicle asset begins with a clear understanding of the product. The source material, available model data and reference imagery should describe the vehicle that customers will actually see in their market.

Make the local detail visible.

Wheels, lighting, badges, interiors and trim options can change between markets. These details deserve their own review before the asset becomes the basis of a film or an interactive experience.

Build for the next use.

The same foundation can support CGI, configurators and virtual showrooms. Each use needs its own preparation, but a consistent vehicle asset keeps the product story connected.
