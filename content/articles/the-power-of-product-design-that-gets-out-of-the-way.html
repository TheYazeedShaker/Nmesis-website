Start with the customer’s questions.

What does the car look like in this color? Which interior belongs to this trim? A configurator should make those questions easy to explore without asking customers to understand the technology behind it.

Connect choices to the real range.

Model and trim logic needs to reflect what is available in the target market. Product information and visual options should tell the same story.

Keep the experience focused.

Readable controls, useful camera views and a clear next step help the customer move from curiosity to a conversation. The experience should remain considered on a phone as well as a larger screen.
